# Lyaf l3adalat (muscle fibres) b d-Darija

Translation of the muscle fibre conversation into **Moroccan Darija** and **Algerian Darija**, written in Arabizi (3 = ع, 7 = ح, 9 = ق, gh = غ, kh = خ, ch = ش). Scientific terms (type I/II, IIa, IIx, mitochondria, myoglobin, ATPase, VO2max...) are kept as they are, the way Darija speakers borrow them from French or English.

**Judgement calls (short notes)**
- *Muscle / fibre*: both versions use **l3adala / l3adalat** for muscle and **lif / lyaf** for fibre. In everyday speech many people simply say *muscle* and *fibre* in French, which is also fine.
- *Weightlifter*: Moroccan uses **li kayrfa3 l7did** ("who lifts iron"); Algerian uses **li yrfed l7did** (*rfed* is the usual Algerian verb for "lift"). Gym talk in both countries often just says *musculation*.
- *Capillaries*: rendered as **l3rou9 r-r9a9** (thin veins/vessels) with *capillaires* in brackets, since there is no common everyday word.
- *Qaf*: Moroccan is written with **9**. Many Algerians pronounce qaf as **g** (e.g. *gal*, *gdem*), but **9** is kept in the Algerian text so it reads across regions.
- *Present tense*: Moroccan uses the **ka-** prefix (*kayjri*); Algerian normally has no prefix (*yjri*) and uses **rah** for "is (now)".

## 🇲🇦 Moroccan Darija

### Chno l-far9 bin l-lyaf dyal l3adalat dyal wa7ed kayjri msafat twila w wa7ed kayrfa3 l7did? L-far9 f d-densité, w kifach ghadi ybanu f l-maqta3 (cross section)?

Akbar far9 li kayban howa 7ejm l-lif. L-lyaf dyal li kayrfa3 l7did ymken twsel l-msa7a dyal l-maqta3 dyalhom l 9rib joj mrat dyal li kayjri, w had t-tkbar kaytjm3 ktar f l-lyaf dyal type II. L-lyaf dyal li kayjri kayb9aw sghar w kifkif ta9riban, w hadchi jouz mn s-sbab 7it l-lif s-sghir 7sen l t-ta7ammul (endurance): l-oxygène kayb9a lih msafa 9sira bach ywsel mn l-capillaire l wast l-khliya.

Ta l-khalta dyal anwa3 l-lyaf mkhtalfa. L-3ddayin l-kbar dyal l-msafat t-twila ghaliban 3ndhom 70–80% dyal type I (slow-twitch) f l3adalat dyal r-rjel li kaykhdmo bihom ktar, walakin riyadiyin dyal l9owa w l-power ghaliban 3ndhom type II ktar bzzaf. Bzzaf mn had t-ta9sim kaytwret, ya3ni r-riyada katkhtar n-nas li 3ndhom had l-khalta, b7al ma t-tadrib kaysn3ha. T-tadrib aslan kaynaqel l-lyaf wast l-groupe dyal type II: j-joj anwa3 dyal t-tadrib kaydfe3o l-lyaf l-asra3 type IIx l IIa li kat9awem l3ya ktar, w daba t-t7wil l-kamel mn type II l type I m7dod.

Mnin n-nas kayhdro 3la "densité" dyal l3adala, ghaliban kay9sdo chno m3ammar dakhel kol lif w 3la jnabo, w j-joj riyadiyin kaykhtalfo f tlata d l-7wayej l-asasiya.

**L3rou9 r-r9a9 (capillaires).** 3nd li kayjri, kol lif m7at bih capillaires ktar, w 7it l-lyaf sghar, l-3adad dyal l-capillaires f kol millimètre carré kaykoun kter bzzaf. Li kayrfa3 l7did kaykhalli 3adad m39oul dyal l-capillaires l kol lif, walakin kol lif kbir bzzaf, dakchi 3lach l-kathafa dyal l-capillaires f l-msa7a kat9ell. L-bahitin kaysmmiw hadchi "l'effet de dilution" (dilution effect).

**Mitochondria w myoglobin.** Lyaf t-ta7ammul 3amrin b l-mitochondria w l-myoglobin, l-protéine li kat7ddar l-oxygène w li kat3ti l3adala "l-7amra" lon dyalha. Ta kaykhzno 7bibat dyal ch-chhem ktar dakhel l-lif bach y7r9ohom b7al l-wa9oud. 3nd li kayrfa3 l7did, l-7ejm dyal l-protéine li kat9bed (contractile) kaykbar asra3 mn 3adad l-mitochondria, dakchi 3lach l-kathafa dyal l-mitochondria f kol wa7da dyal l-7ejm kat9ell.

**Myofibrils.** 3nd li kayrfa3 l7did, ktar l-7ejm z-zayed f l-lif kaykoun myofibrils, l-7zam dyal actin w myosin li kaydirou l9owa. Dakchi 3lach l9owa f kol lif katzid. Kayn chi niqach wach t-tadrib t-t9il kaykbbar 7tta l-ajza2 dyal l-ma w l-ajza2 li ma kat9bdch f l-lif (sarcoplasmic hypertrophy), walakin d-dala2il mkhtalta.

B l-ma3na l-fiziya2i dyal l-kutla f l-7ejm, j-joj ta9riban kifkif. Ns-sij dyal l3adala kaykoun 7wali 1.06 g/cm³ kifma kan t-tadrib. Li kaytbddel howa fach kaytsta3mel dak l-7ejm: li kayjri kay3ti mno ktar l jalb l-oxygène w ntaj t-ta9a, w li kayrfa3 l7did kay3ti mno ktar l l-makina li kat9bed.

F slide 7a9i9i msbough (b7al ATPase wla myosin stain), l-maqta3 dyal li kayjri kayban b7al mosaïque rqi9a w mnddma, ktar mno b lon wa7ed, w l-capillaires bayna bin ta9riban kol lif w lif. L-maqta3 dyal li kayrfa3 l7did kayban b7al damier ghlid dyal lyaf kbar w 3ndhom bzzaf d j-jwaneb (polygones), b jouj d l-alwan mkhtalfin bzzaf, w l-lyaf dyal type II kbar b chkel wad7 3la type I. L-lyaf l-7a9i9iyin homa polygones m7chiyin m3a ba3dhom machi dwayer; ana rsmthom dwayer bach ykounu wad7in.

### Wach type 2 ymken yt7wwel l type 1 w l3aks?

Ah, walakin 9ed ach ymken l-lif yt7wwel kayt3alla9 b l-ittijah w b chno li kaydfe3 t-tbdil. Nou3 l-lif machi tabet mn l-wilada. Nou3 l-lif kayt7dded b l-myosin heavy chain li kaysna3, w hadchi kayt3alla9 bzzaf b l3sab l-7araki (motor nerve) li kaywsslo w kifach had l3sab kaykhdem. 7sen tchouf anwa3 l-lyaf b7al silsila mtwasla, I ↔ I/IIa ↔ IIa ↔ IIa/IIx ↔ IIx, w f l-wast kaynin lyaf "hybrides" li kaysn3o joj anwa3 dyal myosin f nefs l-wa9t.

**Wast type II (IIx ↔ IIa).** Hada howa t-tbdil s-sahel w l-m3rouf. Ta9riban ay tadrib mntadam, sawa2 endurance, l9owa wla sprint, kaynaqel l-lyaf IIx l IIa f chi simanat. Bnadem m3llem (trained) kaywlli 3ndo 9lil bzzaf dyal l-lyaf IIx s-safyin. Ila w9ef t-tadrib kayrja3 kolchi: IIx kayrja3, w b3d wa7d l-modda dyal tadrib t9il mn b3d w9ef (detraining), IIx ymken yfout chwiya l-mustawa l-asli dyalo l modda 9sira.

**Type II → type I.** Hadchi mumkin walakin m7dod 3nd bnadem. Snin dyal tadrib t-ta7ammul kayban bli kayzido chwiya f n-nisba dyal type I, ghaliban mrorran b lyaf hybrides I/IIa, walakin ma kaynach dala2il bzzaf bli l3adala dyal sprinter ymken tdrrbha w twlli l3adala dyal marathonien. T-tajarib 3la l-7ayawanat katbayen bli t-t7wil l-kamel mumkin biologiyan: mnin l-bahitin kayn3asho l3sab dyal l3adala sri3a b-chkel mustamir b pattern bti2 w b fréquence nazla l simanat, l3adala katwlli bti2a ta9riban kollha. T-tadrib l-3adi 3mmro ma kaysifet ichārat b had l-istimrar wla b had t-tatarruf.

**Type I → type II.** S-sabab l-ra2isi howa 3adam l-isti3mal machi t-tadrib. L-b9a f l-frach, t-tajbir (immobilisation) dyal chi tarf, jar7a f n-nkha3 ach-chawki (moelle épinière) w s-safar l l-fada2, kolhom kaydfe3o l-lyaf l j-jiha s-sri3a, 7it n-nachat l-mustamir w l-khfif li kaykhalli type I "bti2" kaytla7. T-tadrib t-t9il dyal l9owa wla sprint ma kayt7wwelch type I l type II b chkel mdmoun. Dok l-lyaf ghaliban ghir kaykbaro.

**L3sab howa li kaytkem.** F s-sittinat, tajarib klassikiya dyal cross-reinnervation rbto l3adala bti2a b l3sab dyal l3adala sri3a w l3aks. Kol 3adala khdat l-khasa2is dyal l3sab j-jdid dyalha, w hadchi bayyen bli pattern dyal n-nachat li jay mn l3sab howa li kayt7dded bih ktar nou3 l-lif.

**L-kbor f s-sen.** L3adala dyal li kber f s-sen kattban ktar type I. Hadchi aslan 7it l-lyaf dyal type II kaysghar w chi wa7din menhom kaymchiw m3a l-motor neurons dyalhom, w l-lyaf li b9aw ghaliban katbennahom motor neurons bti2in, machi 7it kayn tbdil mubachir.

Ya3ni f l-wa9i3, t-tadrib kaynaqel l-lyaf ktar wast l-groupe dyal type II, ay tbdil mn II → I b sabab l-endurance kaykoun sghir, w t-tbdil l-kbir mn l-bti2 l s-sri3 ghaliban kayji mn 9illat l-7araka machi mn rf3 l7did.

### (Wach khassni nkhddem bach ykounu 3ndi lyaf type II ktar mnin nkbar?)

Machi b dbt. Ma3n9ash t9lleb 3la lyaf type II ktar, 7it ma t9derch tbddel n-nisba b chkel mohim. Li mohim f l-kbor f s-sen howa t7afed 3la l-lyaf type II li 3ndek ykounu kbar, khddamin w mrbouṭin b l3sab dyalhom, w f nefs l-wa9t t7afed 3la l-liya9a l-hawa2iya (aerobic fitness) li kat3awen fiha type I.

**Type II homa l-lowlin li kaymchiw m3a s-sen.** Mn wast l-3mer ta9riban, l-lyaf type II kaysgharo ktar bzzaf mn type I, w chi wa7din kaymchiw mnin kaymoutu l-motor neurons dyalhom. Hadchi jouz kbir mn 3lach l-power (9ed ach t9der tdir l9owa b zzerba) kaynqes asra3 mn l9owa m3a s-sen, w l-power howa li khassek bach tnoud mn l-korsi, tTla3 d-drouj wla tchedd rasek mnin ttay7. Chedda dyal l-yedd w l-power dyal r-rjlin dima mrboutin b l-isti9lal, khatar t-tay7an w l-wafayat 3nd l-kbar f s-sen. Dakchi 3lach l-7imaya dyal type II mohima.

**L-liya9a l-hawa2iya mohima b7alha wla ktar.** Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) mn a9wa l-mu2achirat 3la 9ed ach n-nas kay3ichu, w l-lyaf type I m3a l-capillaires w l-mitochondria dyalhom jouz kbir mnha. Ila hmmelti had j-jiha 3la 7sab l-lyaf s-sri3in, ghadi tkoun mbadla khayba.

**Kifach kat7afed 3la type II.** L3adalat kayst3mlo l-lyaf b t-tartib mn s-sghar l l-kbar (size principle), dakchi 3lach type II ma kaytkhdmouch ghir mnin l-majhoud s3ib: t9al t9il, l-akhrin repetitions f série s3iba, wla l-7araka b zzerba w b l9owa. N-nachat l-khfif ktar mno kaykhddem type I. F l-wa9i3, hadchi kay3ni:

* Tadrib dyal l-mo9awama (resistance training) 3la l-a9al joj mrat f s-simana, w twsel 9rib l n-no9ta li ma t9derch tdir fiha répétition okhra. T-tawsiyat dyal Britania kat9ol tkhddem l9owa joj iyyam f s-simana m3a 7wali 150 d9i9a dyal n-nachat l-hawa2i l-mutawassit.
* Chwiya dyal n-niya l-infijariya (explosive), b7al trfa3 t-t9al b zzerba (w nzzlo b t-tsayr), sprint f d-drouj, step-ups wla n-n9iz ila l-mfasel dyalek kayt7mmlo. Hadchi kaywlli mohim ktar m3a s-sen.
* Tmarin hawa2iya b-intidam, w ila amkan m3ahom chi intervals s3ibin, 7it 7tta homa kaykhddmo l-lyaf s-sri3a.
* Protéine kafiya bach t3awen 3la l-7ifad 3la l3adalat, w hadchi kaywlli mohim ktar m3a s-sen.

L-hadaf l-mzyan howa tkoun 9wi chwiya, 3ndek power chwiya w fit chwiya, machi tdfe3 t-tarkiba dyal l-lyaf dyalek l chi tarf. Ila 3ndek chi mrd wla ma tdrrbtich mn chi modda, chawer m3a t-tbib dyalek (médecin généraliste) wla kiné 9bel ma tbda tadrib s3ib wla explosive.

### Chmen nou3 dyal l-lyaf l3adaliya li mnhom kaytkouwen l-fibrome?

L-fibromes machi msnou3in mn l3adalat l-haykaliya (skeletal muscle), dakchi 3lach t-tasnif type I w type II ma kaytba9ch 3lihom. Fibrome dyal r-r7em (b smiya tibbiya leiomyoma) howa namw 7mid (benign) dyal l3adalat l-mals (smooth muscle), l3adalat li ma kat7kkmch fihom b l-irada, li kaynin f 7youṭ r-r7em, l-m3a, l-mtana w l3rou9 dyal d-dem.

Khalaya l3adalat l-mals mkhtalfa bzzaf 3la l-lyaf li kunna kanhdro 3lihom. Sghar w chkelhom b7al l-maghzel (spindle), kol wa7da fiha nawat wa7da, w ma fihomch t-tkhtat (striated) dyal l3adalat l-haykaliya 7it l-protéines li kat9bed ma mrttbinch f wa7dat mnddmin w mtkerrin. Kat9bed b chwiya w t9der tchedd t-tawattur modda twila, w kayt7kkem fiha j-jihaz l3asabi l-istiqlali (autonomic nervous system) w l-hormonat, machi l-icharat l3asabiya l-iradiya.

L-fibrome kaybda mn khliya wa7da dyal l3adalat l-mals f l-myometrium, l-7a2it l3adali dyal r-r7em. Dik l-khliya kattkattar, dakchi 3lach kol fibrome f l-asl howa clone. Chno li kaymiyyez l-fibromes howa bli l-khalaya kaysn3o 7tta kmmiya kbira dyal extracellular matrix, aslan collagène m3a protéines binyawiya okhrin. Had l-matrix ghaliban kaydir jouz kbir mn 7ejm l-fibrome, dakchi 3lach l-fibromes kaykounu 9as7in b7al l-caoutchouc w mnin kayt9t3o kayban fihom chkel mlwi (whorled). T-tkbar dyalhom kaydfe3 fih ktar l-œstrogène w l-progestérone, dakchi 3lach ghaliban kaybanu f snin l-injab w ktar mn mrra kaysgharo b3d s-sen dyal l-ya2s (ménopause).

L-lyaf l-7a9i9iyin houma polygones mlas9in m3a ba3dhom machi dwayer; ana rsamthom dwayer bach ykounou wadh7in.

### Wach type 2 ynajem yetbeddel l type 1 w l3aks?

L-wa7ed li mdarreb (trained) yweli 3ando chwiya bezzaf ta3 l-lyaf IIx s-safyin. **Type II → type I.** Hadi mumkina bassah m7douda 3and bnadem. Snin ta3 tadrib t-ta7ammul yban bli yzidou chwiya f n-nisba ta3 type I, ghaliban yfoutou b lyaf hybrides I/IIa, bassah ma kanch dala2il bezzaf bli l3adala ta3 sprinter tnajem tdarrebha w tweli l3adala ta3 marathonien. ### (Wach lazem nkhdem bach ykounou 3andi lyaf type II ktar ki nekber?)

Machi b dhabt. Ma tqallebch 3la lyaf type II ktar, 3la khater ma tnajemch tbeddel n-nisba b chkel mohim. Wach mohim f l-kbor f s-sen howa t7afedh 3la l-lyaf type II li 3andek ykounou kbar, yakhedmou w marboutin b l3asab nta3hom, w f nefs l-wa9t t7afedh 3la l-liya9a l-hawa2iya (aerobic fitness) li t3awen fiha type I.

**Type II houma l-lowlin li yrou7ou m3a s-sen.** Mn wast l-3omr ta9riban, l-lyaf type II yesgharou ktar bezzaf mn type I, w chwiya menhom yrou7ou ki ymoutou l-motor neurons nta3hom. Hadi parti kbira mn 3lach l-power (9addach tnajem tdir l9owwa b l-khef) ynqes asra3 mn l9owwa m3a s-sen, w l-power howa li lazmek bach tnoudh mn l-korsi, tetla3 d-drouj wella tchedd rouhek ki ttih. Chedda ta3 l-yedd w l-power ta3 r-rjlin dima marboutin b l-isti9lal, khatar t-ti7 w l-wafayat 3and l-kbar f s-sen. Donc l-7imaya ta3 type II mohima.

**L-liya9a l-hawa2iya mohima kifha wella ktar.** Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO2max) mn a9wa l-mu2achirat 3la 9addach y3ichou n-nas, w l-lyaf type I m3a l-capillaires w l-mitochondria nta3hom parti kbira menha. Ila tnsa had j-jiha 3la 7sab l-lyaf s-sri3in, tkoun mbadla machi mliha.

**Kifach t7afedh 3la type II.** L3adalat yesta3mlou l-lyaf b t-tartib mn s-sghar l l-kbar (size principle), donc type II ma yakhedmouch ghir ki ykoun l-majhoud s3ib: t9al t9il, l-akhrin répétitions f série s3iba, wella l-7araka b l-khef w b l9owwa. N-nachat l-khfif ktar mno yakhdem type I. F l-wa9i3, hadi ta3ni:

* Tadrib ta3 l-mou9awama (resistance training) 3la l-a9al zouj khatrat f s-semana, w twasel 9rib l n-no9ta li ma tnajemch tdir fiha répétition okhra. T-tawsiyat ta3 Britania t9oul tkhdem l9owwa zouj ayyam f s-semana m3a 7wayel 150 d9i9a ta3 n-nachat l-hawa2i l-mutawassit.
* Chwiya ta3 n-niya l-infijariya (explosive), kima terfed t-t9al b l-khef (w tnazzlo b t-tawil w b l-control), sprint f d-drouj, step-ups wella n-nagz ila l-mfasel nta3ek ytahmlou. Hadi tweli mohima ktar m3a s-sen.
* Tmarin hawa2iya b intidham, w ila amkan m3ahom chwiya intervals s3ibin, 3la khater 7atta houma yakhedmou l-lyaf s-sri3a.
* Protéine kafiya bach t3awen 3la l-7ifadh 3la l3adalat, w hadi tweli mohima ktar m3a s-sen.

L-hadaf l-mlih howa tkoun 9wi chwiya, 3andek power chwiya w fit chwiya, machi tdez3 t-tarkiba ta3 l-lyaf nta3ek l tarf mn l-atraf. Ila 3andek mard wella ma tdarrebtch mn modda, chawer m3a t-tbib nta3ek (médecin généraliste) wella kiné 9bel ma tebda tadrib s3ib wella explosive.

### Wach mn nou3 ta3 lyaf l3adalat yetkawwen mno l-fibrome?

L-fibromes machi msen3in mn l3adalat l-haykaliya (skeletal muscle), donc t-tasnif type I w type II ma yetba9ch 3lihom. Fibrome ta3 r-r7em (b l-ism t-tibbi leiomyoma) howa namw 7mid (benign) ta3 l3adalat l-mels (smooth muscle), l3adalat li ma tet7akkemch fihom b l-irada, li kaynin f 7youtt r-r7em, l-msaren, l-mtana w l3rou9 ta3 d-dem.

Khalaya l3adalat l-mels mkhtalfa bezzaf 3la l-lyaf li konna nahedrou 3lihom. Sghar w chkelhom kima l-maghzel (spindle), kol wa7da fiha nawat wa7da, w ma fihomch t-tkhtat (striated) ta3 l3adalat l-haykaliya 3la khater l-protéines li t9bed machi mrattbin f wa7dat mnadhmin w mtkarrin. T9bed b chwiya w tnajem tchedd t-tawattur modda twila, w yet7akkem fiha j-jihaz l3asabi l-istiqlali (autonomic nervous system) w l-hormonat, machi l-icharat l3asabiya l-iradiya.

L-fibrome yebda mn khliya wa7da ta3 l3adalat l-mels f l-myometrium, l-7ayt l3adali ta3 r-r7em. Hadik l-khliya tetkattar, donc kol fibrome f l-asl howa clone. Wach ymayyez l-fibromes howa bli l-khalaya ysena3ou tani kammiya kbira ta3 extracellular matrix, asasan collagène m3a protéines binyawiya okhrin. Had l-matrix ghaliban ydir parti kbira mn 7ejm l-fibrome, hadi 3lach l-fibromes ykounou 9as7in kima l-caoutchouc w ki yet9at3ou yban fihom chkel mlawi (whorled). T-tkbar nta3hom ydez3o ktar l-œstrogène w l-progestérone, hadi 3lach ghaliban ybanou f snin l-injab w bezzaf l-khatrat yesgharou ba3d s-sen ta3 l-ya2s (ménopause).
