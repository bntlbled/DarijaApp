# Tarikh l-3abbasiyin (Abbasid history) b d-Darija

Translation of the Abbasid history text and its follow-up into **Moroccan Darija** and **Algerian Darija**, written in Arabizi (3 = ع, 7 = ح, 9 = ق, gh = غ, kh = خ, ch = ش, 2 = ء). It follows the same conventions as [muscle-fibres-darija.md](muscle-fibres-darija.md).

**Judgement calls (short notes)**
- *Names*: people, dynasties and places use their usual Arabic forms (s-Saffa7, l-Mansour, l-Baramika, l-Qahira, Dijla...), with the English form in brackets where readers might not recognise it, e.g. **Dijla (Tigris)**, **l-Baramika (Barmakids)**, **Hulagu (Hulegu)**.
- *Caliph / caliphate*: **khalifa / khulafa2 / khilafa**, as everyone says. *Empire / emperors*: **imbratoriya / abatira**.
- *Figureheads* and *puppets*: rendered as **ghir wajiha** ("only a front") and **dmiyat** (dolls), the usual Darija images; *figureheads* is kept in brackets once.
- *Months*: Moroccan uses **yanayer, yunyu**; Algerian uses the French **janvier, juin**, as each country does in speech.
- *Qaf*: written **9** in both, as in the earlier translation, even though many Algerians say **g**.
- *Present tense*: Moroccan uses the **ka-** prefix (*kay7kem*); Algerian has no prefix (*y7kem*) and uses **rah** for "is (now)".
- *Dangling links*: the two cut-off link fragments in your text are kept as cross-references starting with **chouf:** ("see:"), e.g. *chouf: l-amir l-umawi li hreb l l-Andalus* ("see: the Umayyad prince who fled to Spain"). Spain is called **l-Andalus**, which is how the period is named in Arabic.
- *Follow-up question*: your question in the middle of the text is turned into a heading, and "Answering your follow-up here" is kept as the first line of the answer.

## 🇲🇦 Moroccan Darija

### L-7okm dyal l-3abbasiyin mn tasis Baghdad 7tta s-s9ot f 1258

Ghadi ntba3 l-7okm dyal l-3abbasiyin mn tasis Baghdad, mrorran b t-traju3 dyalhom, 7tta s-s9ot f 1258, m3a l-masader.

L-jawab l-9sir howa bli l-3abbasiyin 7kmo b7al abatira 7a9i9iyin 3la 9rib mn 9arn, w mn b3d dazo ktar mn l-arba3 mya 3am li jat mn b3d ghir wajiha (figureheads), w nas khrin homa li kanu hazzin s-sulta l-7a9i9iya.

**Bni dawla jdida (750 7tta 7wali 809).** Mn b3d ma rbe7 s-Saffa7 f 750, khouh l-Mansour howa li dar t-tathbit l-7a9i9i. 3dem Abu Muslim, l-9a2id li rbe7 lihom th-thawra, f 755. F 762 bna Baghdad, mdina mdawra m7awta b s-sour, w smmaha "Madinat s-Salam" (mdinat s-salam) 3la Dijla (Tigris). Hadchi n9el wast l-imbratoriya l ch-cher9, mn Dimach9 dyal l-umawiyin l jiht Fares, w l-bla9 (court) khda style farisi. L-7koma kanu kaysiyrouha l-wuzara, w l-m3roufin fihom ktar homa 3a2ilat l-Baramika (Barmakids). Harun r-Rachid (786–809) kan howa l-9imma, walakin 7tta howa tqlleb 3la l-Baramika f 803 w 7bes wla 9tel ktar mnhom.

**L-3asr d-dhahabi, w mn b3d chu9u9 (809–861).** Harun 9sem l-wilaya (succession) bin joj wladu, w dakhlo f 7arb ahliya salat mnin dkhel l-Ma2mun l Baghdad f 813. L-7okm dyal l-Ma2mun howa "l-3asr d-dhahabi" l-klassiki dyal t-tarjama dyal l-3ulum l-yunaniya b flous d-dawla. Li ja mn b3do, l-Mu3tasim, n9el l-3asima l Samarra f 836 w bna jaych mn l-3bid l-3askariyin t-turk (Turkish slave soldiers) li kanu mkhlsin lih howa bo7do. Hadchi 7ell mochkil wa7ed w khle9 mochkil kbar mno.

**Fe9dan s-saytara (mn 861 l l-9ddam).** F 861 l-9wad t-turk 9tlo l-Mutawakkil, w rb3a d l-khulafa2 jaw w mchaw f ts3 snin. F nefs l-wa9t l-a9alim bdaw kayb3do: l-Aghaliba f Tounes, t-Tuluniyin f Masr, w s-Samaniyin f ch-cher9. Mn b3d l-Fatimiyin ch-chi3a 3mlo khilafa monafisa w bnaw l-Qahira (Cairo) f 969. (L-Andalus 3mmerha ma kanet dyalhom, chouf: l-amir l-umawi li hreb l l-Andalus.)

**Dmiyat f yeddin s-salatin (945–1258).** L-Buyiyin (Buyids), farisiyin chi3a, khdaw Baghdad f 945, w s-Saljuqiyin t-turk ta7t Tughril khdaw blasthom f 1055. F j-joj d l-7alat, l-khalifa b9at 3ndo l-hiba d-diniya w l-l9eb, walakin s-sultan howa li kan kay7kem. L-khulafa2 rejj3o chwiya mn s-sulta l-7a9i9iya mn b3d 1165, khossousan n-Nasir (1180–1225). Mn b3d Hulagu (Hulegu) khrrb Baghdad f 1258 w 3dem l-Musta3sim (chouf: l-ghazw dyal Hulagu l Baghdad).

**Ma b3d s-s9ot.** Mn 1261, s-salatin l-Mamalik khllaw khalifa 3abbasi bla sulta f l-Qahira bach y-ban l-7okm dyalhom char3i. Hadchi b9a 7tta khdaw l-3thmaniyin Masr f 1517. L-7ikaya li katgoul bli akher khalifa 3ta l-l9eb l l-3thmaniyin hiya ostora tl3at mn b3d.

T2kkdt mn had t-tawarikh f l-ma9al dyal Wikipedia 3la l-khilafa l-3abbasiya (Abbasid Caliphate). Ila bghiti nadra 3amma akadimiya, chouf Cambridge history dyal l-khilafa l-3abbasiya.

### Chno rbe7 s-Saffa7 f 750, chkoun howa Abu Muslim, w chno kan d-dor dyalo m3a l-3abbasiyin?

Ghadi njawbek 3la s-su2al dyalek hna.

**Chno rbe7 s-Saffa7.** Rbe7 l-khilafa b rasha, walakin ma 7arebch 3liha b nefsu. L-atba3 dyalo 3lnoh khalifa f l-Kufa f lekher dyal 749, mn b3d ma jyouch l-3abbasiyin li jayin mn Iran khdaw l-mdina. L-ma3raka l-7asima kanet ma3rakat z-Zab f chamal l-3ira9 nhar 25 yanayer 750, w 3ammu 3abd Allah bnu 3li howa li 9ad j-jaych l-3abbasi tmma. Akher khalifa umawi, Marwan t-tani, tharrem (was routed). Hreb l Masr, w tab3oh 7tta l9awh w 9tloh f knisa f Abusir mn b3d chi chhour. B hadchi salat l-khilafa l-umawiya f kol blasa, ghir f l-Andalus la.

**Chkoun kan Abu Muslim.** 7tta 7ed ma 3aref mzyan fin tzad wla chno smiytu l-7a9i9iya. "Abu Muslim" kan l9eb thawri 3tah lih l-imam l-3abbasi Ibrahim. Trbba f l-Kufa b7al khddam wla 3bd 7tta 7erroh l-3a2ila l-3abbasiya. Mn b3d sifto Ibrahim l Khurasan f ch-cher9 dyal Iran bach y9ud l-7araka s-sirriya dyal mdd l-umawiyin, wakha n-nachitin l-9dam ma 3jbhomch l-7al 7it kan sghir f s-sen w mn asl bsit.

**D-dor dyalo.** Howa r-rajel li dar th-thawra b s7i7. F yunyu 747 rfe3 r-rayat l-k7el dyal l-3abbasiyin f Merv (Marw), w l-ansar dyalo kanu khlit mn l-3rab li ma kanouch radyin w l-iraniyin li dakhlo l l-islam. Khda Merv f lowel dyal 748 w sifet l-9a2id dyalo 9a7taba bnu Chabib l l-gharb 3la Iran. Dok l-jyouch 7llo t-tri9 l l-Kufa w wsslo l n-nasr f z-Zab.

**3lach t9tel.** Mn b3d, 7kem Khurasan 9rib b7al chi malik. Kan kay3ayyen l-wulat, kayjma3 d-draybat, w 7tta drab s-sekka (coins) b smiytu. S-Saffa7 kan khayf mno. F 755 l-Mansour 3iyyet 3lih l l-bla9 w khlla l-7rras y9t3oh mnin 3tahom ichara, w mn b3d lo7 j-jetta dyalo f Dijla. Bzzaf mn l-atba3 dyalo amnu bli ghadi yrje3 b7al l-mahdi (messiah), w thawrat b smiytu tl3o f Iran l 3chrat d s-snin mn b3d.

### Wach rbe7 s-Saffa7 f 750, chkoun howa Abu Muslim, w wach kan d-dor ta3o m3a l-3abbasiyin?
