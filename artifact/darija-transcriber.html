Darija Transcriber

Arabizi with English underneath, Moroccan vs Algerian, and French and English words marked where speakers switch.

Paste what was said, in Arabic script, Arabizi or a mix, one line per segment. Claude transliterates, tags and translates it, and the dialect check runs on the result.

Examples:

Claude isn't available in this view, so only the Mina podcast tab works here. Open the page in claude.ai while signed in to use this tab.

Audio needs the full app running on a computer, because pages like this one can't use the microphone or a speech engine. To run it: download DarijaApp from GitHub, add an Anthropic key plus an ElevenLabs or OpenAI key to .env, and run ./run.sh. Then open localhost:8000 to record or upload audio. Everything after speech recognition works the same as the Paste a transcript tab here.
