    Instagram's terms don't let apps download other people's reels automatically, so save the video first and bring it here:

    1. If the creator allows it, tap Share → Download (or … → Save) on the reel.
    2. Otherwise, screen-record the reel with sound on.
    3. Then pick the file in Audio / video, or on Android use Share → Darija Transcriber after adding this app to your home screen.
